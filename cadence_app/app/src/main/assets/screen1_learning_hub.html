Why Minor Chords Feel Somber

It hinges on the flattened 3rd interval. A Major 3rd spans 4 semitones (bright resonance), while a Minor 3rd narrows down to 3 semitones, imparting that melancholic tension.

C to E♭ = 3 Semitones (Minor)
C to E = 4 Semitones (Major)
