Producer Secret: The Circle is Clockwise Energy

Moving clockwise along the Circle of 5ths adds sharps and harmonic tension (V of V). Counter-clockwise adds flats (subdominant motion), producing warm, relaxed resolutions favored in neo-soul and ambient compositions.
